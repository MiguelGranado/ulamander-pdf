# Security and privacy

Ulamander PDF is designed to keep your documents on your device.

- Files are opened, edited and saved locally. They are never uploaded.
- The only network requests are: (1) activation / re-validation of your personal key with `licencias.ulamander.com`
  (key + random device identifier, never document content) and (2) a check of this repository's Releases page to tell you
  when a new version exists.
- No analytics, no advertising identifiers, no third-party trackers.

## Reporting a vulnerability
Please email **info@ulamander.com** with the subject “Security — Ulamander PDF”, describing the issue and how to reproduce it.
Do not open a public issue for security problems. We aim to reply within 72 hours.

## Verifying downloads
Download installers only from this repository's [Releases](../../releases) or from [pdf.ulamander.com](https://pdf.ulamander.com).
